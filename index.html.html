Habit Busters! 🦸‍♂️💡

Hi there! Need ideas for your project? You're in the right place! ✨
Look at these examples to help you think. Remember the Habit Loop: Cue ➡️ Routine ➡️ Reward. Let's go!

Biting Nails 💅

Cue (the trigger): Feeling nervous or bored. 😟

Routine (the action): I bite my fingernails.

Reward (the good feeling): It gives me something to do and distracts me.

My Plan to Stop! 🚀

  • Put bad-tasting polish on nails.
  • Keep a fidget toy in my pocket.

Late-Night Phone Time 📱

Cue (the trigger): Getting into bed. 🛌

Routine (the action): I scroll on my phone for a long time.

Reward (the good feeling): It's fun and I see what friends are doing.

My Plan to Stop! 🚀

  • Charge my phone in another room.
  • Read a book for 15 minutes instead.

Eating Junk Food 🍿

Cue (the trigger): Feeling hungry after school. 😋

Routine (the action): I grab a bag of chips or candy.

Reward (the good feeling): The yummy, sugary, or salty taste!

My Plan to Stop! 🚀

  • Ask parents to buy healthy snacks.
  • Drink a glass of water first.

Forgetting Homework 📚

Cue (the trigger): Getting home from school. 🏡

Routine (the action): I play video games or watch TV right away.

Reward (the good feeling): I get to relax immediately (right away).

My Plan to Stop! 🚀

  • Do homework first, then play.
  • Reward myself with game time *after* homework is done.

Clothes on the Floor 👕

Cue (the trigger): Taking off clothes for bed. 🌙

Routine (the action): I drop clothes on the floor.

Reward (the good feeling): It's fast and easy.

My Plan to Stop! 🚀

  • Put my laundry basket close by.
  • Make it a game: "shoot" clothes into the basket.

Interrupting People 🤫

Cue (the trigger): Getting an exciting idea while someone talks. 💡

Routine (the action): I say my idea right away.

Reward (the good feeling): I don't forget my idea!

My Plan to Stop! 🚀

  • Take a deep breath and wait.
  • Silently count to three before I speak.

Cracking My Knuckles 💥

Cue (the trigger): My hands feel stiff or I'm feeling fidgety.

Routine (the action): I push my fingers to make them "pop".

Reward (the good feeling): The popping sound and feeling of release.

My Plan to Stop! 🚀

  • Squeeze a stress ball instead.
  • Gently stretch my fingers.

Slouching at My Desk 🚶

Cue (the trigger): Sitting down to do homework or play games. 🪑

Routine (the action): My back and shoulders curve forward.

Reward (the good feeling): It feels comfortable and relaxed.

My Plan to Stop! 🚀

  • Put a sticky note on my screen that says "Sit Tall!"
  • Set an alarm to check my posture (how I'm sitting).

Procrastinating (Putting things off) ⏳

Cue (the trigger): Getting a big school project. 😫

Routine (the action): I decide to do it "later" and do something fun.

Reward (the good feeling): I avoid the hard work for a little while.

My Plan to Stop! 🚀

  • Break the project into tiny, easy steps.
  • Do just one tiny step right away (the "5-Minute Rule").

Complaining a Lot 😩

Cue (the trigger): Something doesn't go my way. 🌧️

Routine (the action): I complain about the problem.

Reward (the good feeling): It feels good to let out my frustration.

My Plan to Stop! 🚀

  • Challenge myself to find one good thing about the situation.
  • Instead of complaining, think of one possible solution.

Comparing Myself on Social Media 🤳

Cue (the trigger): Opening Instagram or TikTok. 😟

Routine (the action): I scroll and see others' "perfect" lives and feel bad about mine.

Reward (the good feeling): I feel connected, but it also makes me feel down.

My Plan to Stop! 🚀

  • Remind myself it's a "highlight reel," not real life.
  • Unfollow accounts that make me feel bad.

Skipping Breakfast 🍳

Cue (the trigger): Waking up late or feeling rushed in the morning. 🏃

Routine (the action): I run out the door without eating anything.

Reward (the good feeling): I get a few extra minutes of sleep.

My Plan to Stop! 🚀

  • Prepare something the night before (like overnight oats).
  • Grab something simple like a banana or granola bar.

Drinking Too Much Soda 🥤

Cue (the trigger): Eating a meal, feeling thirsty, or seeing a vending machine.

Routine (the action): I grab a soda or energy drink.

Reward (the good feeling): The sugary taste and the caffeine boost.

My Plan to Stop! 🚀

  • Always carry a reusable water bottle.
  • Try flavored seltzer water instead for the fizz.

Gossiping About Others 🗣️

Cue (the trigger): Being in a group of friends and feeling bored.

Routine (the action): I say something negative about someone who isn't there.

Reward (the good feeling): I feel included (part of the group) and it's exciting.

My Plan to Stop! 🚀

  • Change the subject to something positive.
  • Say something nice about that person instead.

Negative Self-Talk 🧠

Cue (the trigger): Making a mistake or getting a bad grade.

Routine (the action): I think, "I'm so stupid," or "I can't do anything right."

Reward (the good feeling): None, really. It just becomes a bad mental habit.

My Plan to Stop! 🚀

  • Ask myself, "Would I say this to my best friend?"
  • Replace the bad thought with "It's okay, everyone makes mistakes."

Being a "Couch Potato" 🛋️

Cue (the trigger): Getting home from school with free time.

Routine (the action): I lie on the couch and watch shows for hours.

Reward (the good feeling): It's super relaxing and entertaining.

My Plan to Stop! 🚀

  • Plan a fun activity, like a walk with a friend.
  • Do just 15 minutes of exercise before screen time.

Using Filler Words ("Like," "Um") 💬

Cue (the trigger): Feeling nervous while talking or trying to think.

Routine (the action): I say "like," "um," or "you know..." too much.

Reward (the good feeling): It fills the silence and gives my brain a second to think.

My Plan to Stop! 🚀

  • Practice pausing silently instead of saying "um."
  • Ask a friend to gently point it out when I do it.

Messy Backpack / Locker 🎒

Cue (the trigger): The bell rings at the end of class.

Routine (the action): I quickly shove all my papers and books into my bag.

Reward (the good feeling): I can leave class faster and talk to my friends.

My Plan to Stop! 🚀

  • Use colored folders for each subject.
  • Take 30 seconds to clean out my bag every night.

Being Late for Things ⏰

Cue (the trigger): It's time to leave for school or to meet friends.

Routine (the action): I try to do "one last thing" before I go.

Reward (the good feeling): I feel like I'm not wasting any time.

My Plan to Stop! 🚀

  • Set an alarm for 10 minutes *before* I need to leave.
  • Get my clothes and bag ready the night before.

Spending Money Without Thinking 💸

Cue (the trigger): Feeling bored, seeing an online ad, or getting allowance.

Routine (the action): I buy something I don't really need.

Reward (the good feeling): The excitement of getting something new.

My Plan to Stop! 🚀

  • Wait 24 hours before buying anything over $10.
  • Make a budget and track my spending.
